Instrument samples for TuneJournal's synthesized ("MIDI") playback of thesession.org settings.

A subset of the FluidR3_GM soundfont as rendered to per-note MP3s by
https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts (the layout abcjs loads).
FluidR3_GM by Frank Wen, licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 license
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

Fetched by app/scripts/fetch-soundfont.js; the instruments and pitch ranges are listed in
app/src/lib/domain/synth.ts.
